# Sub character: FEED v2 scenario (draft for review)

## Reading the lyrics again

The song is not "the sub character becomes the hero". It goes:

1. **Watching** (V1): always looking at YOU's back → "can I ever be like you" → inferiority, the heart goes cold.
2. **Hating** (Pre): I'm not the hero, just an extra → you looked perfect, so I hated you for no reason → sorry, I couldn't help it.
3. **Envy** (C1): we're not alike, you shine, everyone likes you, you look happy → I'm a loser → am I a bad person? → I wanna be you.
4. **Turning inward** (V2): I started to hate myself → whose fault? you → no, you did nothing wrong, it's because I'm me → **"how do you feel, hero? are you hurting too?"** (the first time she thinks of YOU as a person) → tangled → sorry for only hating you, but it's hard to erase.
5. **Giving up** (C2): I'm not important → an ugly loser's montage → an ending already decided → will my story be thrown away?
6. **Night** (Bridge): no one said anything, but I compare us alone → think the worst of you, then sigh with relief → I know I'm awful → I tried to fix it, it doesn't work.
7. **The same chorus again** (FC): the same words, but by now we know her.
8. **Outro**: my story in the end: not important → tell me someone it isn't → I know it's meaningless → **"I don't care. Even as a loser I can hang on"** → **"Help me, hold my hand, because you're the hero."**

The ending is not victory. She accepts she's the sub character, and for the first time she reaches out to YOU instead of watching. YOU takes her hand.

## Story device: two photo albums

The screen is ME's phone, and there are two kinds of pictures.

- **YOU's public feed** (`mem_*`, bright, posed): YOU at the centre with friends. This is how the world sees him.
- **ME's private camera roll** (`pov_*`, taken from far away, through glass, from behind): her own photos of YOU. This is how she sees him.
- **ME herself** appears as a person in two places:
  - her profile picture (`me_portrait`, a girl), from the intro on;
  - her own selfies (me_cry / angry / envy, which she deletes; me_smile, which she posts and gets 0 likes).

  So she is on screen as "me" but never inside the scenes with him until the last hand.

## Motion spine: one continuous scroll (the 1:20 grammar all the way through)

- The whole video is one scroll that never resets. What you liked at 1:20 becomes the rule. Speed follows the song: slow in the verses, fast in the choruses, stopped in the bridge.
- **Scene changes:**
  - Zoom into a post: it becomes the page, the scroll carries on inside it, then zoom back out.
  - The coral dot carries us across.
  - No cuts, no pop-in/pop-out one by one.
- **Removed:**
  - Repeated tiles and loop resets (`% 340`).
  - The stories that popped in one card at a time (C2).
  - The per-line colour wipes and giant type in the final chorus.
- **Bridge and final chorus keep the same objects** as acts 1–2 (posts, captions, chips, DMs). This fixes the "different texture after 2:30".
