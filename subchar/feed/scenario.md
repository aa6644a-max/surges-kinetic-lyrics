# Sub character: FEED scenario (one take, jelly motion)

**Story:** ME is a small coral dot (a notification) in YOU's feed.
- The dot never leaves the screen. Every scene is the same dot turning into the next object.
- It falls in, gets shoved aside by YOU's story circle, becomes a lens, a banner icon, a slider knob, a spinner's core.
- It bursts into hearts, stretches toward YOU and snaps back.
- It becomes a selfie mirror, a deleted DM, a story timeline, the one dot left when everything goes quiet.
- In the end it uploads its own post, and becomes the period in "서브캐릭터."

**Motion:** every shape is a spring.
- Squash & stretch with volume kept, smear along the velocity, overshoot and settle, trailing colour layers.
- Objects fall and land with a wobble.
- Page changes are shapes: a disc that grows into the next page, a slit that opens, a diagonal plane that slides in. No fades, no cuts.

**Lyrics:** small, inside the objects (captions, banners, chips, input boxes, bubbles), each syllable on its sung time. Big only at the two climaxes.

**Palette:**
- Ink `#12131F`, Cream `#FCEBDD`, White `#FFF9F1`, Grey `#BDB7C9`
- ME coral `#FF6B6B`, YOU violet `#5A3CF0`
- Yellow `#FFD23F`, Peach `#FFB36B`, Teal `#62B6B7`, Mauve `#6D5B80`, Deep `#23243F`, Pink `#FF8FB1`

**Type:** Pretendard Variable, plus Inter Tight Variable for English.
